# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all people who participate in the Minecraft Command Maker project. We pledge to create an environment of mutual respect where all contributors, users, and community members feel valued and heard.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and inclusive of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being constructive in criticism and feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping new contributors feel welcome

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments related to personal characteristics
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Hate speech or slurs
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the person reporting an incident.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

- Project repositories
- Issue trackers and pull request discussions
- Community forums and chat channels
- Official project events
- Public spaces when representing the project
